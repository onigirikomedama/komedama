Privacy Policy

KOMEDAMA handles personal information received through inquiries according to the following policy.

Information and purpose

We use the name, email address, inquiry details and other information you provide to reply to and handle your inquiry. Please do not include personal information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

Management and retention

The KOMEDAMA administrator manages inquiry information and takes measures, including access controls, to prevent unauthorized access and disclosure. We aim to delete personal information held in Google Forms responses and related emails within one year after an inquiry is resolved. Information required by law is retained for the necessary period.

Sharing information

As a rule, we do not share personal information with people other than the administrator or provide it to third parties, except with your consent or where required by law. We use the external services described below to receive and handle inquiries.

External services

This site is planned to be hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Forms for inquiries and Google Fonts for fonts. Accessing these services may send information such as your IP address and browser details to their providers. Please consult their privacy policies. When you open external map or social media links, the destination service’s policies apply.

Requests about your information

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, please contact us using the inquiry form. We will verify your identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Changes

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in law or operations and will post updates on this page.
