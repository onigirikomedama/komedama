Privacy Policy

KOMEDAMA handles personal information received through inquiries or social media photography according to the following policy.

Information and purpose

We use the account name, inquiry details and other information you provide through Instagram direct messages to reply to and handle your inquiry. Please do not include personal information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

Management and retention

The KOMEDAMA administrator manages inquiry information and takes measures, including access controls, to prevent unauthorized access and disclosure. We aim to delete personal information in inquiry records managed by KOMEDAMA within one year after an inquiry is resolved. Data held by service providers is handled according to their policies. Information required by law is retained for the necessary period.

Sharing information

As a rule, we do not share personal information with people other than the administrator or provide it to third parties, except with your consent or where required by law. We use the external services described below to receive and handle inquiries.

External services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts for fonts. Inquiries are received through Instagram direct messages. These messages are sent and received on Instagram, provided by Meta, and are subject to its privacy policy. Accessing these services may send information such as your IP address and browser details to their providers. Please consult their privacy policies. When you open external map or social media links, the destination service’s policies apply.

Requests about your information

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, please contact us through Instagram direct messages. We will verify your identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Photos for social media

Only with your permission to publish, we may also take photos on our shop smartphone and share them on KOMEDAMA’s Instagram and TikTok. Participation is optional. You can enjoy a free photo with our staff on your own smartphone without taking part in our social media photos.

Photos taken for our social media are used for publication and related management and are managed by the KOMEDAMA administrator. Please contact us through Instagram direct messages if you would like a published photo removed.

Bring your own photos to Omocha no Tsuchida for acrylic keepsake production. KOMEDAMA does not collect photos from your smartphone or forward them to the shop. Please ask the shop how it handles photos provided for production.

Changes

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in law or operations and will post updates on this page.
