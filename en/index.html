Take Home a Memory

Bring your photo to nearby Omocha no Tsuchida (おもちゃの土田) to have an acrylic keychain or badge made.

Turn Your Photo into a Keepsake.

  1. Take a photo using your own smartphone.
  2. Bring your smartphone with the photo to Omocha no Tsuchida (おもちゃの土田).
  3. Request an acrylic keychain or acrylic badge at the shop.

Planned price: ¥2,000 per item, including tax, for either option
Production time: approximately 10 minutes

Available from our first stall on November 21, 2026.

Items are made and collected at Omocha no Tsuchida. We do not make or hand over these items at the KOMEDAMA stall, or collect your photo files there.

Photos for Our Social Media

With your permission to publish, we may also take photos using our shop smartphone to share on KOMEDAMA’s Instagram and TikTok. Participation is optional. You can enjoy a free photo with our staff on your own smartphone without taking part in our social media photos.
